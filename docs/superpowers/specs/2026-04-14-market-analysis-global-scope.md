## 3. HYPERFIT's Unique Position

**No competitor combines all three:** AI meal recognition + contextual AI coaching + real-time computer vision workout tracking.

Key differentiators:
1. **MediaPipe workout tracking** — non-existent in consumer apps, most viral feature
2. **Contextual AI coach** — knows meals, workouts, goals, history
3. **German-first** — YAZIO now French-owned (Groupe SEB). HYPERFIT = "die deutsche KI-Fitness-App"
4. **BLS food database** — 7,140+ lab-verified German foods, 138 nutrients (free API)

## 6. Key Threats

- MyFitnessPal acquired Cal AI (March 2026) — incumbents buying AI fast
- Apple/Google could add native nutrition tracking
- New AI-native startups entering weekly

## 7. Regulatory (DACH)

- **DSGVO:** Health data is "special category" — explicit consent required, fines up to €20M
- **BFSG:** Accessibility law effective June 2025, fines up to €100K
- **Impressum:** Legally required for all commercial German websites
- **Widerrufsrecht:** 14-day cancellation right for subscriptions
